01 — THE BERNSTEIN MOMENT
More than a coffee stop

Made with heart.
Served with joy.

Café Bernstein was opened in 2013 and takes its name from the owner's lucky stone. The café describes its philosophy as “Freude verbreiten” — spreading joy. Many of its recipes and cakes are created in its own kitchen, with an emphasis on fresh, quality ingredients and options for different tastes.

“Freude verbreiten” — spreading joy, one plate at a time.
